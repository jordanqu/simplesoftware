Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

Simple Software is an independent software consultancy operated by Jordan Quartermain (ABN not yet registered). Our contact email is jordan.default@gmail.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information that you share with us when you visit this website or engage our services.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact information — your name and email address when you reach out to us.
  • Project details — information you voluntarily share about your business or project requirements.
  • Usage data — standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected automatically. We do not use third-party analytics on this site.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or project request.
  • Communicate about ongoing or potential projects.
  • Issue invoices and process payments where applicable.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Payments

Payment processing for services is handled by Stripe, a PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor. When you make a payment, your payment card details are collected and processed directly by Stripe and are never stored on our systems. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

5. Data retention

We retain your contact and project information for as long as necessary to fulfil the services you requested, or as required by applicable law (e.g. tax record-keeping obligations).

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Withdraw consent to communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at jordan.default@gmail.com.

7. Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. Basic session or preference cookies may be set by the hosting platform (GitHub Pages) but contain no personal information.

8. Third-party links

This website may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies independently.

9. Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect information you share with us from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. All communication with this site is encrypted via HTTPS.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects when the policy was last revised. Continued use of this website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at jordan.default@gmail.com.
